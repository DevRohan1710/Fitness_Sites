01 — About The Factory

Built like a factory floor.
Trains like one too.

Bond Fitness Factory (BFF) is a gym on the first floor of Sundrop Co-Operative Society, opposite Biswa Bangla Sarani in Action Area I, Newtown. The space is laid out around a working floor of strength machines, a cable/functional rig, free-weight benches and conditioning tools — not a boutique studio, a factory for output.

The gym is wheelchair accessible and welcomes an LGBTQ+ friendly community, and currently carries a 4.4-star rating across 214 Google reviews.

02 — Training Zones

What you'll actually lift, pull & push.

Zones are based on the equipment on the floor — no invented class schedule, just the stations you'll find when you walk in.

Strength Training

Selectorized machines and plate-loaded stations for structured, progressive strength work.

Cable & Functional Rig

A multi-station cable trainer built for pulldowns, presses and full-range functional movement.

Free Weight Benches

Dedicated bench stations for presses, isolation work and free-weight fundamentals.

Conditioning Floor

Open mat space with battle ropes for high-output conditioning and finishers.

Cardio Deck

A row of treadmills along the window line, set on turf flooring beside a mural wall.

Boxing Corner

A dedicated heavy bag station set against a dark accent wall near the entrance.
